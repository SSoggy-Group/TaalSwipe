TaalSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Overview

TaalSwipe is a Dutch language quiz game. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

Data Stored on Your Device

TaalSwipe stores gameplay progress, settings, scores, achievements, and unlocked in-game items locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you choose to remove the app or clear app data.

Data Collection

TaalSwipe does not collect names, email addresses, contact details, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers.

Children

TaalSwipe is suitable for students and younger players. The app does not include chat, account creation, ads, or real-money purchases.

Permissions

The app may use sound and vibration for gameplay feedback. It does not need microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the support contact listed on the App Store or Google Play listing.
