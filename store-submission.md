## Short Description

Swipe through Dutch language challenges and improve spelling, grammar, slang, and brand-name knowledge.

## Full Description

TaalSwipe is a fast Dutch language quiz game. Players swipe through short challenges, build streaks, earn XP, unlock cosmetic themes, and practice spelling, grammar, straattaal, Dunglish, Van Dale-style words, and brand-name recognition.

No account is required. Progress and settings are stored locally on the device.
